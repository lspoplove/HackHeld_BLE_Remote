How key mapping works

Keyboard: one key plus optional Ctrl, Shift, Alt or Win/Cmd. Actions depend on the focused app and its shortcuts.

Media: playback, volume and track controls. Phone mute depends on the phone and player; it is not the ringer or Do Not Disturb switch.

Labels: 1–12 printable English/ASCII characters. The OLED overview shows the first 10. This is a name, not text to type.

Repeat: available on direction buttons only. A and B fire on release; holding A+B for 1.2 seconds remains the Back gesture and cannot be reassigned.

Saving affects only the selected profile. No macros, scripts, firmware uploads or browser-triggered key presses are provided. Setup closes after 10 minutes without a page request; unsaved browser edits are not stored.

Press B on the remote to exit at any time. Wi-Fi turns off; saved mappings stay on the device.
